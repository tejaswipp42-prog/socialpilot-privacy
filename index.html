SocialPilot AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

SocialPilot AI helps users generate and manage social media content using AI.

2. Information We Collect

The application may process content you provide, such as articles, prompts, and generated social media posts. If you connect a social media account, the application may receive account information and authorization tokens permitted by that platform.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to generate content, support social media account connections, and provide requested publishing features.

4. Third-Party Services

SocialPilot AI may use third-party services such as Google Gemini and LinkedIn. Their own privacy policies and terms apply to their services.

5. Account Security

Users should never share their social media passwords with SocialPilot AI. Account connections should use the official authorization process provided by the relevant platform.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may request deletion of their personal information by contacting the application administrator. Requests will be handled according to applicable requirements and the data the application actually stores.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the SocialPilot AI application administrator.
