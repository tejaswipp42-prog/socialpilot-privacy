SocialPilot AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

SocialPilot AI is an application designed to help users generate and manage social media content using artificial intelligence.

2. Information We Collect

The application may process text, articles, prompts, and social media posts that users provide. If users connect a social media account, the application may receive account information and authorization tokens permitted by that platform.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to generate content, support authorized social media account connections, and provide features requested by users.

4. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services such as Google Gemini and LinkedIn. These services have their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Account Security

Users should never provide their social media passwords directly to SocialPilot AI. Account connections should use the official authorization process provided by the relevant platform.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may request deletion of personal information by contacting the application administrator. Requests will be handled according to applicable requirements and the information the application actually stores.

7. Data Sharing

Information should only be shared with third-party services when necessary to provide requested features or when otherwise required by applicable law.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, please contact the SocialPilot AI application administrator.
